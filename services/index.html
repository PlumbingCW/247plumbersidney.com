Sidney homes and businesses call us for two kinds of work. Some problems cannot wait, like a burst pipe, a sewer backup or a water heater that has started to leak. Others are planned, like a new water heater, a repipe or a softener.

The pages below explain each service in plain terms: what causes the problem, how we find the cause, what the repair options are and what drives the price. Emergency services say what to do while you wait, and the planned services list what to prepare before the visit.

How to choose

Not sure which service you need?

Start with the symptom. Water that will not stop points to burst pipe repair, a slow drain to drain cleaning, and a bill that jumped to leak detection. If you cannot tell, call the 24/7 line at (855) 575-2890 and describe what you see, and we will point you to the right visit.
