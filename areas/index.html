Our home zone is Sidney, ZIP 45365 and 45367, including Port Jefferson and the rest of the surrounding area in Shelby County. The towns below each have their own page because their water systems, storm history and housing differ from Sidney's.

Each page lists the town's ZIP codes, the water and sewer notes we could confirm from public sources, and the services callers there ask about most. Where we could not confirm a fact, such as water hardness, the page says to test your tap or ask the village instead of guessing.

Beyond these towns

Outside the five town pages

Communities such as Kettlersville, Lockington and Houston sit close to the towns above and are served on the same 24/7 line. Larger places farther out, like Wapakoneta in Auglaize County, Troy in Miami County and Bellefontaine in Logan County, are outer-ring calls, so phone and ask before you assume anything.

Whichever town you call from, the first step is the same. Describe what is happening, get the safe first moves, and hear the price before work starts. If you are unsure which page fits your street, the services list explains each repair and the phone answers any hour of the day or night, including weekends and holidays.
