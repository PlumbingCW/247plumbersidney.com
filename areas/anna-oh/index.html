Village water

What the village wells tell you about your tap

Anna's three wells draw from an aquifer that the state review rates as having low susceptibility to contamination. Total chlorine was about 0.98 ppm in the report we read, and fluoride measured 1.25 ppm, which the village says comes from natural deposits.

The report does not print a hardness number. If you see white crust on fixtures or on a water heater element, test your tap or ask the village office at 209 W. Main St. for the latest report. Scale is one of the usual reasons a tank loses capacity over the years.

What we fix

Calls from Anna, starting with the water heater

Water heaters that leak or go cold

Hot water failures are the most common call, and they are often urgent when a tank is leaking. Water heater repair covers elements, thermostats and valves, and our line is open around the clock for it.

Tanks past their service life

Tank heaters often last roughly 8 to 12 years. When a tank leaks at the seam, replacement is the realistic fix, and we discuss tank size before installing.

Split or frozen supply pipes

With January lows near 18 F at the Sidney weather station, pipes in cold walls can freeze. Burst pipe repair begins with stopping the flow.

Drains that stop moving

Kitchen and bathroom clogs, and the occasional main line blockage, go to drain cleaning.

Water that appears from nowhere

A spongy floor or a meter that keeps turning with everything off points to a hidden leak, which our leak detection visit locates.

Act fast

If water is moving right now

Main valve

The main shutoff is usually near where the water line enters the house, and a quarter-turn or wheel valve there stops everything.

Electricity

If water reaches outlets, appliances or the panel, switch off the breaker only if you can do so dry. Otherwise stay out and call 911.

Tank power

For an electric water heater, turn off its breaker so the elements do not run dry. For a gas unit, set the control to off.

Common questions

Do you cover every ZIP code in Anna?

Anna has only one ZIP, 45302, and we cover it from our Sidney base just to the south. If you are on the edge of the village, give us the street and we will confirm that a crew can reach you.

Is emergency service available in Anna?

Yes. We take emergency calls around the clock, including nights, weekends and holidays. A leaking water heater or a burst pipe in Anna gets dispatched from Sidney, and we tell you how to limit damage while the plumber travels.

Is Anna's water safe for my water heater?

The village reports a low susceptibility rating for its aquifer and treats with chlorine. The report does not print hardness, so test your tap if scale concerns you, and flush a tank periodically to clear sediment.

Who do I call for a plumbing permit in Anna?

Plumbing permits are issued by the Sidney-Shelby County Health Department at 937-498-7249. The Shelby County Building Department at 937-658-6220 covers building permits in Anna, so ask each office about your own job.

How do I know if my tank is leaking or just sweating?

Dry the base and watch it. Condensation fades when the room warms up, while a real leak returns and often leaves rust. If water keeps appearing, shut off the supply and call.

Do you replace a water heater the same day?

When a standard tank is on hand, a same-day appointment is often possible. Otherwise we order the right size and tell you the timing. You get a clear price before work starts.
