Local knowledge

Why Sidney's rules shape the work

Plumbing in Sidney touches three public offices. The City of Sidney runs the water system and the sewer main. The Sidney-Shelby County Health Department issues plumbing permits and registers plumbers each year. For commercial work, the Ohio Construction Industry Licensing Board licenses the contractor.

Knowing which office owns which question saves time. A leak between the meter pit and the house is the owner's to fix, a blocked City main is the City's, and a job that is more than a minor repair may need a permit. Ohio's plumbing code was updated effective March 1, 2024, and model plumbing codes such as the UPC and IPC are the background the trade works from.

The City also runs an inflow and infiltration program for sewer laterals, and the City's water reports are public. When a job touches those programs, we point you to the right City office rather than guess. That habit saves you a second phone call and keeps a repair from being redone because a rule was missed.

Check anyone

How to verify any plumber, including us

  • Ask whether the plumber is on the Sidney-Shelby County Health Department's registered plumber list, and call 937-498-7249 to confirm.
  • For commercial work, look up the contractor's state plumbing license with the Ohio Construction Industry Licensing Board.
  • Ask for the price in writing before work starts, and ask what is and is not included.
  • Ask whether your job needs a permit. Minor repairs are treated differently from replacements and new work.

Yes. The phone line is answered 24 hours a day, every day, including holidays. If the problem is not urgent, we book the next open appointment and tell you what to do until then.

Do you give free estimates?

We give a clear price before work starts, after the plumber has seen the problem. We do not call that a free estimate, and we do not quote exact numbers over the phone, because the price depends on what the plumber finds.

Do you serve commercial customers?

Yes, shops, restaurants and offices in Sidney can call the same line. Commercial work in Ohio requires a state license, and we explain how to verify one.

You can write to contact@247plumbersidney.com for non-urgent questions. For anything involving water, sewage or gas, call the 24/7 line instead, because email is not monitored like the phone.
